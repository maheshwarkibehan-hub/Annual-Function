ACT 01 17:30 IST · MAIN AUDITORIUM

The Whispering Forests

Van Vandana · Eco-Symphonic Overture

The gala commences with "Van Vandana" — an original eco-symphonic suite conducted by Maestro D. K. Sharma. Orchestrating traditional bamboo flutes, acoustic strings, and woodland resonance, celebrating four decades of living in harmony with Mother Earth.

Conductor Maestro D. K. Sharma
Ensemble Senior Eco-Vocalists
Theme Van Vandana (D Major)
ACT 02 18:15 IST · AMPHITHEATRE STAGE

Panchatatva & Kinetic Rhythm

Dance of the 5 Elements: Earth, Water, Fire, Air, Space

An evocative classical and folk choreography paying homage to Panchatatva — the sacred elements of nature. St. Joseph's Senior Sec. School dancers weave intricate earthen mudras, traditional pot balances, and rhythmic footwork to depict the fragile balance of life.

Choreography Smt. Ananya Sen
Rhythm Cycle Teentaal & Bhoomi Cadence
Troupe Senior Green Guild
ACT 03 19:00 IST · AUDITORIUM GRANDE

The Thespian Stage

Original Environmental Drama: "The Last Banyan"

Set against the shadow of encroaching urbanization, this student-penned drama follows three generations fighting to preserve an ancestral banyan grove. A stirring emotional journey of eco-activism, biodiversity conservation, and planetary stewardship.

Direction Kabir Mukherjee ('27)
Cast Size 34 Student Thespians
Theme Conservation & Hope
ACT 04 19:50 IST · CONVOCATION HALL

Green Honours & Valedictory

Planetary Stewardship Citations & Presidential Address

Conferment of the Green Guardian Medals, the Principal's Eco-Scholastic Quill, and the Cock-House Environmental Shield. Featuring the keynote address by Hon. Chief Guest Dr. Rajeshwari Varma, dedicated to youth climate stewardship and sustainable sciences.

Chief Guest Dr. Rajeshwari Varma
Laureates 48 Eco-Scholastic Laureates
Trophy The Green Cock-House Shield
ACT 05 20:40 IST · CENTRAL AUDITORIUM LAWNS

Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam

All-School Earth Anthem & Emerald Illumination

The entire student body and faculty unite under starlit skies for the school's Earth Anthem "Prakriti Vandana". The evening culminates in an architectural emerald laser choreography and synchronized cold-spark stage illumination celebrating our living planet.

Ensemble 600 Students & Faculty
Illumination Emerald Lasers & Cold Sparks
Earth Anthem Prakriti Vandana (One Earth)
