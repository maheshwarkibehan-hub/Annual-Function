The Thespian Stage

Set across three historical eras, this student-penned drama examines the fragility of ambition and the eternal pursuit of wisdom. Featuring student actors, bespoke period costuming, and cinematic stage illumination.

Direction Kabir Mukherjee ('27)
Cast Size 34 Student Thespians
